## Overview

[Boopable](https://boopable.io) is a practical, no-marketing-fluff pet vaccination reminder service and digital passport app designed specifically for busy pet owners.
It targets pet owners who travel frequently and need a reliable way to manage their pets' health documents.

## Core features

**Digital Storage** <br/>
Store digital pet passports and comprehensive vaccination records.

**Smart Reminders** <br/>
Automatically send notifications and reminders when vaccination boosters are due.

**Document Scanning** <br/>
Scan physical pet passports directly into the application.

**Vaccination Renewal** <br/>
Facilitate the renewal process for pet vaccinations.

**International Support** <br/>
Includes support for US-issued health certificates.

## Technical specifications

**Platforms** <br/>
iOS and Android.

**Tech Stack** <br/>
Built using React (Next.js), Prisma, Postgres, tRPC, and Capacitor.
