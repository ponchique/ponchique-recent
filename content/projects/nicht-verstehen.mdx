The website is here: [nichtverstehen.berlin](https://nichtverstehen.berlin)

A practical guide for Ukrainians and Russian speakers moving to Berlin. I designed and built it: the content, the UX and the interface, from housing and banking to sorting trash.

![Nicht Verstehen on mobile](/images/nicht-export.webp)

Articles are made to be scanned on a phone. Dark theme, big headings, colour-coded sections.

![Recycling cheat sheet](/images/recycling-complete.png)

Complex topics become one-page visual cheat sheets, like this guide to Berlin's recycling bins.

![Home page wireframes](/images/nicht-wireframes-home.png)

![Category page wireframes](/images/nicht-wireframes-category.png)

Wireframes for the home and category pages across phone, tablet and laptop, with search and a "Didn't find what you were looking for?" prompt.
